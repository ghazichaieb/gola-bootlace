# Names and logos

The code of Gola Bootlace is free software under the GPL (see `LICENSE` and `ADDITIONAL-TERMS.md`): you may use, change and share it. The **names** "Gola",
"Gola Services" and "Gola Bootlace", and the **logos** in `branding/`, are not part of that licence. They identify the original program and its maker, and belong
to Gola Services SUARL.

## You may

- Say that your product **is based on**, **works with** or **is compatible with** Gola Bootlace, and link to this project.
- Share unmodified copies, builds and packages (a distribution's package, a container image, a mirror) under the name Gola Bootlace.
- Use the name to talk about the program: reviews, articles, tutorials, issues, questions.

## You may not

- Call a **modified version** "Gola Bootlace", or give it a name that includes "Gola" or is easily mistaken for it, or use the logos for it. A fork needs its own name and its
  own branding. (The licence lets you fork and change anything else, including the web interface; the attribution line stays, see `ADDITIONAL-TERMS.md`.)
- Present your product, or a service you sell around it, as made, approved or supported by Gola Services when it is not.
- Use the names or logos in a domain name, a company name or an app-store listing in a way that suggests the original.
